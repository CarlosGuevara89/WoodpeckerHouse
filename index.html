Welcome

A Nature Lover's Paradise

Experience the Magic of Osa Peninsula

Located amid lush tropical forests and near fascinating natural attractions such as Corcovado National Park and Isla del Caño Biological Reserve, Woodpecker House is the perfect destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

Our location in Rancho Quemado de Osa offers guests a quiet and serene retreat away from the hustle and bustle of daily life. With comfortable amenities, warm hospitality, and stunning natural scenery, Woodpecker House is the ideal place to relax, reconnect with nature, and explore the wonders Costa Rica has to offer.
